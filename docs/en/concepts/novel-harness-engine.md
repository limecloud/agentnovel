# Novel Harness Engine

`Novel Harness Engine` is the v0.1.2 structure-control layer for Agent Novel. It treats harness as a steering, constraint and execution framework: the author uses it to control story structure, character arcs, reader experience and post-publication constraints.

It is not autopilot. It produces diagnostics, options, impact analysis, feedback mappings and proposals. Durable manuscript changes still go through proposal, approval and apply flows.

Core layers:

- Story Harness: plot spine, chapter order, scene function, information release and foreshadowing chains.
- Character Harness: character goals, beliefs, relationships, abilities, wounds and state transitions.
- Reader Harness: golden-three survival, pacing, hooks, reader feedback, drop-off risk and timeline repair.

Lifecycle modes:

- `sandbox`: before publication, structural rewrites are allowed through controlled proposals.
- `timeline`: after publication, published chapters are read-only and future chapters carry repairs.
